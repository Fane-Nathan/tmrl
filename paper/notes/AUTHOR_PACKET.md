### Policy ambiguity that needs attention

- [Preparing Your Manuscript](https://socs.binus.ac.id/icodit/post/preparing-your-manuscript/) limits manuscript-preparation AI assistance and says AI-generated data, references, figures or images are prohibited.
- [AI Tools](https://socs.binus.ac.id/icodit/post/ai-tools/) discusses brainstorming and language assistance, prohibits fabrication/falsification, and permits certain labeled illustrative images and disclosed image processing.
- These pages differ on images and disclosure placement/title. The more specific preparation instruction provides a clear disclosure location. Obtain clarification for any use that depends on the more permissive image wording or on the interpretation of implementation assistance.
