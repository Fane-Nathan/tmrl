## Key results (all numbers in the PDF come from generated macros)

- HalfCheetah, delay: reactive controller adapts (+689) but loses clean return (-1,390); transformer does not adapt and loses clean return (-2,496).
- Retained replay reduces every HalfCheetah clean-condition loss (all 8 seeds each, Holm p = 0.0625 = floor), but shrinks the reactive delay gain (D = -284 [-567, -1]); 15 of 16 replay effects across both tasks have positive means.
- Critic diagnostic: under delay the source critics overvalue delayed histories (+171/+268); replay keeps this gap larger (+62 [16, 109], +90 [41, 139]).
- Ant: sources stand (|forward velocity| <= 0.007 m/s); faults cost little; every arm, clean controls included, dips in the first 2,000 steps.
- The pilot's +98 transformer delay gain does not hold with 8 seeds (-87 at 4,000 steps).
