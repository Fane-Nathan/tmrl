About ICoDiT 2026
Join global researchers, innovators, and industry leaders at ICoDiT 2026, where cutting-edge ideas in computing, digital technologies,
and intelligent systems come together. This premier forum showcases the latest breakthroughs and fosters collaboration across academia and industry.
Present your research, case studies, or work-in-progress to a global audience, gain expert feedback, and expand your professional network.
Selected papers will be published in the conference proceedings and indexed in leading digital libraries, giving your work wide visibility and impact.
Proceedings & Indexing: All accepted papers will be submitted for publication in Springer Nature’s Communications in Computer and Information Science (CCIS), which is indexed by Scopus, subject to meeting the publisher’s scope, editorial, and quality requirements.
