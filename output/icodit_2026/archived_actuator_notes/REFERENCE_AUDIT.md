# Reference recency audit

Source: bibliography printed on pages 7-8 of complete paper.pdf. This checks years as printed; primary-source bibliographic metadata has not yet been independently verified.

## Result

- Total references: **30**.
- Dated 2022-2026: **7/30 = 23.3%**.
- Even including every 2021 reference: **13/30 = 43.3%**.
- Conference requirement: **at least 70% from the last five years**. Both interpretations currently fail.

At a fixed total of 30 references, at least **21** must qualify. Using 2022-2026, this means replacing/reworking at least **14** older entries with genuinely relevant recent sources if the total stays 30. Simply adding 14 references would give 21/44, which still fails. For a changed total N, recompute the threshold as ceil(0.70 x N). Preserve necessary foundational citations; revise the literature coverage rather than padding the bibliography.

The conference does not define the five-year cutoff precisely. Confirm whether 2021 papers qualify. Software access dates are not publication years; cite an actual release/version accurately rather than changing an old source year to make it look recent.

Rule source: [Preparing Your Manuscript](https://socs.binus.ac.id/icodit/post/preparing-your-manuscript/).

## Literature revision priorities

1. Current in-context/meta-reinforcement-learning methods and their actual transfer evaluations.
2. Recent robust/adaptive continuous control under actuator delay and other faults.
3. Recent offline-to-online and demonstration-assisted RL, with relevant comparisons to the implemented recipe.
4. Sequence/recurrent replay, representation interference and high-update-ratio training stability.
5. Accurate versioned software/dataset citations for the environments and framework.

For each candidate reference, verify the paper exists, author list/title/year/venue/DOI, the exact supported claim, and whether its experimental setting actually matches the claim made in this paper. No new references have been invented or inserted into the source.
